## Purpose

Not every customer is an equal candidate for Oktane. Reps have limited outreach capacity between now and September 22, and international attendance requires longer lead times than domestic (visas, travel budgets, internal approvals). This strategy defines the profile of a customer who is (a) likely to say yes, (b) likely to get approval to travel, and (c) likely to convert attendance into pipeline or expansion after the event.

Use this profile to prioritize your book of business before you start outreach. A focused list of 10 well-qualified accounts will outperform a blast to 100.

The working ICP for global Oktane attendance:

- **Active evaluation or expansion motion.** Customer is in a renewal window within [PLACEHOLDER] 2 quarters, has an open expansion opportunity, or is evaluating a competitive displacement. Oktane accelerates deals already in motion; it rarely creates them from nothing.
- **Identity is a named initiative.** Zero trust program, CIAM rebuild, workforce consolidation, M&A integration, or a compliance deadline (GDPR audit, FedRAMP milestone, [PLACEHOLDER] regional regulation). If identity isn't on their internal roadmap, the travel case won't clear their leadership.
- **Champion with travel authority or access to it.** Director level or above, or a senior architect whose manager has discretionary budget. Individual contributors without sponsorship rarely get international travel approved.
- **Account size threshold.** [PLACEHOLDER] $XXk ARR minimum, or strategic-account designation, or named in the White Glove Target Accounts list (Strategy 2).
- **No blocking constraints.** Check for hard blockers early: PubSec gift/ethics limits, works-council travel restrictions in EMEA, visa timelines that can no longer be met (see the regional pages for specifics).
