## Purpose

"Come to our conference" is a weak pitch. "The healthcare identity track covers clinician single sign-on and the [PLACEHOLDER] new patient-identity roadmap, and two of your peer organizations are presenting" is a strong one. Vertical Value Blueprints are the bridge between Oktane's agenda and a specific customer's world.

Each blueprint maps a vertical's identity priorities to concrete Oktane content: tracks, sessions, roadmap topics, and peer-networking opportunities. Reps use them to write invites that sound like they were written by someone who understands the customer's industry — because they were.

This matters double for international customers. A customer justifying long-haul travel to their leadership needs a business case, and "sessions directly relevant to our [PLACEHOLDER] compliance deadline" is a business case in a way "great keynotes" is not.

## How to use this

1. Identify the customer's vertical, then read the matching page under the Verticals section of this hub before writing any outreach.
2. Lead with **their** priority, not our agenda. Open with the initiative you know they have (from account notes, QBRs, support history), then connect it to two or three named pieces of Oktane content.
3. Use the conversation starters on each vertical page verbatim if you want — they're written to be spoken.
4. For mixed-vertical accounts (e.g., a healthcare payer with a large fintech arm), pick the vertical that matches your **champion's** role, not the company's SIC code.
5. If the agenda hasn't locked and you can't name sessions yet, name tracks and roadmap themes instead, and promise a follow-up with the specific sessions once published. That follow-up is a free second touch.
6. Combine with the regional page: vertical tells you **what** to say, region tells you **how** to say it.
