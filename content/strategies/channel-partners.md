## Purpose

In most international markets, the partner knows the customer better than we do. Distributors, resellers, and SIs in LATAM, APJ, and parts of EMEA often hold the day-to-day relationship, speak the language, and understand the local travel-approval culture. A partner-delivered Oktane invitation frequently lands where a direct Okta email would be ignored.

This strategy turns partners into an Oktane recruitment channel: co-branded invitation kits, partner attendance incentives, and partner-organized customer delegations (a partner bringing [PLACEHOLDER] 5-10 of their customers as a group, which also solves the "traveling alone to Las Vegas" hesitation many international attendees have).

The secondary benefit: partners who bring customers to Oktane and walk the floor with them come home better enabled and more committed to the Okta practice.

## How to use this

1. Check partner coverage on every international target account **before** direct outreach. If a partner owns the relationship, route the invite through them — going around your partner burns goodwill for one registration.
2. Brief the partner properly: share the vertical blueprint and regional guidance for the account, and agree on who says what. The partner delivers the invite; you (or the partner manager) provide air cover and any exec-meeting offers.
3. Offer the delegation model to partners with several qualified customers in one market. Group travel lowers the psychological and logistical barrier significantly for first-time international attendees.
4. Register partner-sourced attendees with the [PLACEHOLDER] partner-source tracking code so the partner gets credit and the incentive structure works.
5. Loop in the partner manager for anything involving incentives or MDF — do not improvise financial commitments to partners.

:::region latam
LATAM runs heavily through distributors and regional resellers, and partner-led delegations have been the [PLACEHOLDER] most effective LATAM recruitment motion in prior years. Practical points: co-branded materials must be available in Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil is Portuguese — do not send Spanish materials to Brazilian customers); partners can often bundle Oktane travel with customers' existing US trips; and partner-organized groups materially help attendees with visa justification, since a formal invitation plus a defined business itinerary strengthens the visa application. Start delegation conversations with LATAM partners no later than [PLACEHOLDER] early August.
:::

:::region apj
In much of APJ the sale is relationship-driven and the partner is the relationship. A direct invite from an unknown Okta rep can read as presumptuous, especially in Japan and Korea. Let the partner make the introduction, and where possible have a senior Okta leader extend the formal invitation afterward — seniority of the inviter matters.
