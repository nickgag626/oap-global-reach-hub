## Purpose

Reps recruit better when they can show, not describe. A customer on a call who sees the actual Oktane agenda builder, a two-minute lab preview, or last year's keynote highlights understands the value faster than any email can convey it. This strategy packages a set of "demo" assets — not product demos, but conference-experience demos — that any rep or CSM can run in under ten minutes without special prep.

The confidence angle matters as much as the assets. Many CS reps and partner sellers have never attended Oktane themselves and feel unequipped to pitch it. These resources close that gap: if you can share your screen, you can make the case.

## How to use this

1. Watch the enablement recording once before your first recruitment call. Ten minutes of prep removes most of the hesitation reps report.
2. On live calls, default to the screen-share walkthrough rather than describing the event. Pull up the agenda, filter to the customer's vertical track, and let them react to real session titles.
3. Use the highlight reel as a pre-call or follow-up asset, not as the centerpiece — video sent cold gets skimmed; video after a good conversation gets forwarded internally, which is what you want.
4. Keep the FAQ crib sheet open during calls. Fumbling on "how much is a pass" or "when does registration close" undercuts the pitch. If you don't know an answer, say you'll follow up same-day — and do.
5. For customers evaluating whether to send multiple people, walk through the agenda showing parallel tracks — it makes the multi-attendee case visually obvious.
6. Time-zone note for global calls: the walkthrough works fine over video, but check the regional page for norms on formality and whether a partner should co-host.
